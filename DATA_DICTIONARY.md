# Data dictionary
- Date: reporting date.
- Children apprehended and placed in CBP custody*: daily intake count.
- Children in CBP custody: active CBP custody count.
- Children transferred out of CBP custody: daily transfer flow to HHS.
- Children in HHS Care: active HHS care count.
- Children discharged from HHS Care: daily discharge flow.

Counts are aggregate. They are not linked to individual children or cohorts.
